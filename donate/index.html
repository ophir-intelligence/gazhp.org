Your Impact

Where Your Gift Goes

Every contribution funds programs that build Zambia's healthcare capacity. Our three core programs are Addiction & Mental Health, Cardiovascular Care, and Family Medicine & Primary Care. Read about our health programs in Zambia

Training & Capacity Building

Support continuous medical education and skills development for Zambian healthcare workers. Knowledge that lasts a lifetime.

Specialist Care & Telemedicine

Expand access to specialist healthcare through diaspora collaboration and telemedicine technology. Bridging the gap between rural Zambia and global expertise.

Equipment & Health Systems

Strengthen healthcare facilities and emergency response with critical equipment and systemic support that transforms how care is delivered.

Track Record

What GAZHP Has Done

Highlights of GAZHP's work with partners and the Zambian diaspora, from our published news posts. Each one links to the full story.

2021

US$5,000 toward 40 tonnes of equipment

GAZHP contributed US$5,000 to a fundraiser arranged by the Africa Diaspora Development Institute (ADDI) to sponsor a 40-tonne load of medical equipment and PPE for the University Teaching Hospital in Lusaka.

Read the story: GAZHP joins ADDI to ship 40 tonnes of medical equipment to Zambia

July 2021

Shipping donated PPE from the UK

GAZHP funded the shipment to Lusaka of 35 boxes of visors and masks donated by PLL Care Services of Oxfordshire, UK, with help from community fundraising.

Read the story: 35 boxes of PPE shipped from the UK to Zambia

September 2021

PPE for Zambia's Ministry of Health

PPE donated by UK-based Zambians, including 20,000 surgical masks and 6,000 face shields, was shipped by GAZHP for handover to the Ministry of Health. On 17 September 2021 GAZHP representatives in Lusaka handed PPE and pulse oximeters to the Ministry, asking that they go to rural hospitals and health centres.

Read the update: oxygen concentrators, PPE and equipment en route to Zambia · The handover: GAZHP hands over PPE to Zambia's Ministry of Health

September 2021

Oxygen concentrators and pulse oximeters

GAZHP purchased ten 10-litre oxygen concentrators and handed them to the airline for freight to Zambia. Lifebox donated 200 pulse oximeters after GAZHP contacted it.

Read the update: oxygen concentrators, PPE and equipment en route to Zambia

2021

Resuscitation training manikins for UNZA

GAZHP president Dr. Joseph Yikona and resuscitation practitioner Kevin Brown, both of West Suffolk Hospital, arranged for the hospital's surplus simulation manikins to go to the University of Zambia School of Medicine in Lusaka.

Read the story: UNZA School of Medicine to receive resuscitation training equipment

2025–2026

Mental health essay competitions

In 2025 GAZHP sponsored a Youth Mental Health Essay Competition for university students in Zambia, with cash prizes and six-month shadow internships for the top three finalists. In 2026 GAZHP and The YANA Foundation opened a nationwide competition.

2025 competition: Youth Mental Health Essay Competition · 2026 competition: Nationwide University Mental Health Essay Competition 2026

Donor FAQ

Questions About Giving

Is my gift tax-deductible?

GAZHP is a registered US 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Gifts from US taxpayers are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. If you pay tax outside the US, the rules differ, so please check with your tax advisor.

Can I give monthly?

Yes. Monthly giving is available at checkout: choose the monthly option when you pick your amount. You can cancel a monthly gift at any time by emailing info@gazhphealth.org. You can also use the Manage your gift page. See how cancelling works.

Which payment methods can I use?

The payment box at the top of this page always lists the options available right now. Today you can give by card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay or Google Pay through Stripe's secure checkout. Zambian Visa and Mastercard cards work too.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Online payments are processed by our payment providers (Donorbox, Stripe, PayPal or DPO Pay, depending on the option you choose). Your card, bank and mobile-money details go directly to them and never reach GAZHP. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

How do I get a receipt?

We send a receipt to the email address you give at checkout. Please keep it for your records. If it hasn't arrived, check your spam folder or email info@gazhphealth.org.

Can I get a refund?

If something went wrong, for example you gave the wrong amount or paid twice, email info@gazhphealth.org with your name, the date, the amount and the payment reference. We review every request, and approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Full details are in our Terms & Refund Policy.

Who stands behind GAZHP? GAZHP was registered as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Delaware, USA, in December 2019. It is governed by a Board of Directors: healthcare and finance professionals across the diaspora and in Zambia. Meet the board · Read our story

Still have a question? Email info@gazhphealth.org. We typically respond within 2–3 business days.
