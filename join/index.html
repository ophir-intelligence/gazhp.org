Become a Member

Join a global community of Zambian healthcare professionals committed to leadership, mentorship, professional growth, and improving healthcare outcomes for our communities.

Pick your tier, then choose a secure payment method. GAZHP is a registered US 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Membership dues paid by US taxpayers may be tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

What do members get?
  • Professional: full voting rights, access to all GAZHP programs, CME and training opportunities, and the members-only online community.
  • Student: the GAZHP member network, educational webinars and events, mentorship, and career guidance.
  • Corporate: recognition across GAZHP, sponsorship and engagement opportunities, and program partnership access.
