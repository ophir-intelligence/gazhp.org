Manage Your Monthly Gift

Update the card you give with, or cancel a monthly gift or automatic membership renewal.

Get a secure link by email

Enter the email address you used when you set up your monthly gift or membership. If it has a monthly gift or automatic renewal paid by card on this website, we'll email it a secure link to manage it with our card payment provider, Stripe.

To change or cancel a monthly gift or automatic membership renewal, email info@gazhphealth.org with your name, the email address you used and, if you have it, the payment reference. We'll take care of it and confirm by email.

Good to know

Cancelling stops future payments. It doesn't refund payments already made. If a payment was taken that you didn't expect, see our refund policy.

This page finds monthly gifts and automatic renewals paid by card through this website. If you set up your gift another way, for example through Donorbox, or you'd rather talk to a person, email info@gazhphealth.org and we'll help. See how cancelling works.

We only use your email address to look up your gift and send you the link. Read our Privacy Policy.
