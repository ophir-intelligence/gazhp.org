Nationwide University Mental Health Essay Competition 2026

Entries closed 31 August 2026 • Winners announced 10 October 2026 • GAZHP Health & The YANA Foundation

To mark World Mental Health Day 2026, GAZHP Health and The YANA Foundation opened a nationwide mental health essay competition for students across Zambia's public and private universities and higher education institutions. Entries closed on 31 August 2026. Winners will be announced on World Mental Health Day, 10 October 2026.

More than an academic contest, the competition gives young people a safe, meaningful platform to reflect on and share their experiences of mental health — whether personally lived or observed in others — while helping to break the stigma that still surrounds it.

"Prioritising Student Wellbeing: Breaking Stigma and Navigating Mental Health Pressures in Zambian Higher Education"

What it's about

For university students, the higher education environment is their workplace — academic demands, performance expectations, financial pressures and uncertainty about the future all mirror the challenges of professional life. Entrants were invited to explore the cultural, social and systemic pressures affecting student mental health in Zambia, and to propose practical, community-focused solutions institutions could adopt.

Who could enter

The competition was open to students at all recognised public and private higher education institutions across Zambia — including the University of Zambia (UNZA), the Copperbelt University (CBU), Mulungushi University, Eden University, Cavendish University and others. To protect participants, essays could be submitted under a pseudonym, with identities managed confidentially by a designated competition coordinator.

Important — Eligibility Verification

The competition was open to students currently enrolled at a recognised public or private higher education institution in Zambia. Proof of student status was not required at the time of submission. However, shortlisted finalists will be required to provide a valid student ID card or other official proof of current enrolment before prizes are awarded. Failure to provide satisfactory proof of eligibility by the stated deadline may result in disqualification and selection of the next highest-scoring entrant.

Recognition

The three highest-scoring entries will receive tiered cash prizes, together with mentorship through the combined professional networks of GAZHP Health and The YANA Foundation:

  • 1st prize: US$100
  • 2nd prize: US$75
  • 3rd prize: US$50

In addition:

  • Every eligible participant will receive a co-branded digital Certificate of Participation for their academic portfolio and CV.
  • Winning essays will be published across GAZHP Health's international platforms and The YANA Foundation's national media channels.

How entries were submitted

  • Essay length: up to 1,000 words
  • Pseudonym option: entrants could write under a pseudonym if they preferred
  • Submission deadline: 31 August 2026 (entries are now closed)
  • Winners announced: World Mental Health Day — 10 October 2026

Entries closed on 31 August 2026, and new submissions are no longer being accepted. Entries were submitted by email to info@gazhphealth.org. Questions about the competition? Get in touch.

GAZHP runs initiatives like this to amplify young Zambian voices and build a culture of openness and support around mental health. You can also read about our 2025 Youth Mental Health Essay Competition.
