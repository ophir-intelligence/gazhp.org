Opportunity Entries closed · Winners 10 Oct 2026

Nationwide University Mental Health Essay Competition 2026

For World Mental Health Day 2026, GAZHP Health and The YANA Foundation ran a nationwide essay competition across Zambia's universities, inviting students to break the stigma and reflect on mental health pressures in higher education. Entries closed on 31 August 2026; winners will be announced on 10 October 2026.
